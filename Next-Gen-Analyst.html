GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Where is my employee data stored?

By default, data stays in this browser or a CSV file you link. If Supabase is configured, authenticated organization members share an organization-scoped cloud dataset protected by row-level security.

Can I open my data in Excel?

Yes. Import and export use CSV, which can be opened in Excel and other spreadsheet apps.

Does the free trial save my changes?

No. Trial mode uses sample records, and edits are temporary. Full access saves data in the current browser or linked CSV.

Can I pay online right now?

No. Online checkout is paused. Use the pricing enquiry to ask about full access; the page will not charge you.

Is this suitable for sensitive HR records?

This local browser app is not production-grade security. Avoid storing sensitive or regulated employee information in it.
